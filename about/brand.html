Colour palette

A civic indigo and deep-navy system keeps the identity clear, contemporary and nonpartisan.

Civic indigo — #533AFD
Deep navy — #1C1E54
Ruby accent — #EA2261
Cool canvas — #F6F9FC

Typography specs

Use Noto Sans Tamil with Arial or another neutral Latin sans serif. Match visual weight, line height and contrast across both scripts.

Photography guidance

Use documentary photographs of real participants, classrooms, neighbourhoods and field work. Obtain consent and avoid generic stock imagery.

Do / don't

Do

Use clear space, approved artwork, accessible contrast and the full organisation name on first reference.

Don't

Stretch the mark, change its proportions, add party colours or imply endorsement of a candidate or party.

Name-usage rule

Use “Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Centre for Ethics and Politics” on first reference and “the Centre” thereafter. Chapters may use “Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Centre for Ethics and Politics — Campus Chapter”; do not imply endorsement of a candidate or party.
