College chapters

College chapters

Start a practical public-leadership community on your campus. Chapters help students understand institutions, practise civic problem-solving and learn from people doing the work.

What a chapter is

A chapter is a student-led learning group with a faculty contact, a published charter and a term plan focused on public institutions and local problem-solving.

What chapters actually do

Each term combines foundational learning, one field exercise, a public conversation and a reflective showcase.

Month 1

How local government works

Month 2

Campus and neighbourhood issue mapping

Month 3

Conversation with a public practitioner

Month 4

Field project and public reflection
