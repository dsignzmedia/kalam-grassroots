For campaign teams

Turn strategy into disciplined action.

Great campaigns are built by teams, not slogans.

This path is for the people behind the candidate: researchers, organisers, communicators, data teams, volunteer leads and operations managers.

Learn how to translate a political goal into a focused plan, a reliable field operation and a campaign that respects voters and the people doing the work.

Campaign team roadmap

From useful contributor to campaign leadership.

Campaign skill is a combination of judgement, coordination, communication and reliable systems—not only speed.

Get your start
