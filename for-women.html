Women in public leadership

Lead on the strength of your work.

Ability should matter more than access.

Women often enter public life with less access to networks, money, safety and family support. The Abdul Kalam Fellow journey combines political capability with practical support for the barriers that should never decide who gets to lead.

Build your public voice, a strong peer network and a record of work that belongs to you.

Begin with the Foundation Course
