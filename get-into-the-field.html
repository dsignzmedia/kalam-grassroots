Our approach

Useful work builds public trust.

This stage is for campaign operators and local organisers who want a professional craft and a reliable way to turn daily activity into public value.

01 · People

Organise around responsibility

Give volunteers clear roles, useful support and a reason to return—not just a list of tasks.

02 · Process

Build a field rhythm

Plan, act, report and review in short cycles so the team learns while the work is moving.

03 · Technology

Make evidence usable

Use simple data systems and communication tools that support judgement instead of replacing it.

  1. 1Map
  2. 2Listen
  3. 3Plan
  4. 4Act
  5. 5Review

Democratisation means giving more people the tools to participate meaningfully. Accountability means every task has an owner and every claim has evidence.

What progress looks like at Level 2

A dependable field contribution.

You do not need a title. By the end of this stage you should be able to:

  • Own a defined task and see it through
  • Work with residents or volunteers and report what happened
  • Improve the next cycle using evidence
