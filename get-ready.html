Get ready to enter public life.

You are interested but unsure where to begin. Start by understanding the system, finding the issue that matters to you and taking one low-risk first step.

Level 1Explore public leadership

Our approach

Curiosity becomes a credible first step.

This stage is for people who care about a public problem but are not ready to choose a political role. We make entry easier without making the work shallow.

01 · People

Find your public

Listen to residents, practitioners and people already affected before deciding what the problem is.

02 · Process

Learn how decisions move

Understand the institution, the responsible office and the route from concern to action.

03 · Technology

Use tools to see clearly

Use accessible maps, explainers and records to turn confusion into a grounded starting point.

  1. 1Listen
  2. 2Learn
  3. 3Choose
  4. 4Begin
  5. 5Reflect

Democratisation means lowering the cost of understanding public life. Everyone should be able to see how the system works and where they can contribute.

What progress looks like at Level 1

A considered first commitment.

You do not need to have decided anything. By the end of this stage you should be able to:

  • Name one public issue you care about
  • Explain who is responsible for it
  • Choose a practical way to learn or contribute, without overpromising
