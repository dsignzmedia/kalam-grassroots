How progress is judged

Capability becomes credible when it can be demonstrated.

Across the Academy and 100-day Lab, progress rests on work that can be reviewed—not status, connection or visibility.

I

Organisation building

Leadership begins with building durable teams.

Evidence looks like: a team that keeps working without you.
II

Citizen impact

Public work must create a verifiable benefit.

Evidence looks like: a change people in your community can confirm.
III

Reliability

Consistency matters more than occasional visibility.

Evidence looks like: showing up steadily, week after week.
IV

Learning

Reflection and learning keep practice improving.

Evidence looks like: noticing what worked, and doing it better.
Yourprogress

Verifiable

Every claim needs evidence that someone else can check.

A living practice

Progress reflects ongoing work, not an accumulated past.

Equal voice

No member's endorsement carries extra weight. A senior leader's support counts exactly the same as a new volunteer's.

Your privacy on the journey

Your certification can be shared. Your learning record stays protected.
