Level 4Build and mentor

Our approach

Leadership compounds when authority is shared.

This stage is for organisers who already coordinate work and are ready to build teams, mentor new leaders and make progress less dependent on themselves.

01 · People

Spot and develop talent

Give emerging leaders real responsibility, useful feedback and room to form their own judgement.

02 · Process

Make leadership repeatable

Build routines for delegation, review, conflict, learning and succession that survive individual personalities.

03 · Technology

Share institutional memory

Use accessible systems so knowledge, decisions and relationships belong to the team—not one gatekeeper.

  1. 1Notice
  2. 2Invite
  3. 3Delegate
  4. 4Coach
  5. 5Step back

Democratisation means distributing knowledge and authority. Reproduction means a leader is measured partly by the leaders they help create.

What progress looks like at Level 4

A team that does not depend on one person.

Leadership is measured partly by the leaders you help create. By the end of this stage:

  • Responsibilities are distributed
  • Emerging leaders make decisions
  • Learning is documented, and succession is part of the work
