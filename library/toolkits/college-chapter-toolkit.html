College Chapter Toolkit

A practical starter bundle for creating a structured campus space for civic learning and useful public work.

4 items in this bundle · Built by the Centre’s learning team · Last updated September 2026

What’s inside

Template

Chapter charter template

Define the chapter’s purpose, roles, decision rules and relationship with the college.

Guide

Four-step startup guide

Move from a small founding group to approval, recruitment and the first useful activity.

Template

Community norms

Set expectations for respectful discussion, non-partisanship, safety and shared responsibility.

Template

Recruitment flyer templates

Invite students with a clear purpose, accessible language and a concrete first meeting.

Why this toolkit is built this way

Campus groups often begin with enthusiasm but no shared operating structure. The charter and norms establish trust; the startup guide turns intent into action; and the recruitment templates make the first invitation specific and welcoming.

The toolkit keeps the chapter focused on learning and public contribution rather than personality, hierarchy or partisan mobilisation.

Who this is for

For students, faculty mentors and college administrators establishing a Centre campus chapter.
