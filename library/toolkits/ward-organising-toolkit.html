Toolkit Track: Get Into the Field Theme: Organising TA/EN

Ward Organising Toolkit

Four practical tools to help you understand a ward, meet residents and build a reliable local team.

4 items in this bundle · Built by the Centre’s learning team · Last updated September 2026

What's inside

Template

Door-knock script

A respectful opening, useful questions and a clear way to record the next step.

Worksheet

Ward map worksheet

Map streets, institutions, community groups, public assets and priority issues.

Template

Volunteer sign-up sheet

Capture contact details, interests, availability, consent and an initial responsibility.

Checklist

Weekly review checklist

Review commitments, evidence, blockers and named next actions every week.

Why this toolkit is built this way

Ward organising often fails because contact lists, local knowledge and weekly follow-up live in separate places. This toolkit links a conversation script, a simple ward map, a volunteer sheet and a weekly review so a small team can learn and act together.

It deliberately avoids complex software and campaign messaging. The first goal is a reliable local practice: listen, map, assign, follow up and review. Teams can add specialist tools only after that routine works.

Who this is for

For first-time organisers, resident groups and small campaign teams beginning without an established ward structure.

Get the toolkit

The reasoning above is everything — reading it is free and requires nothing. The packaged files (editable templates, printable worksheets) download as one bundle through a lightweight account, so you're not re-entering your details next time.
