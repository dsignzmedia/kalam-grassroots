Women Candidate Toolkit

Practical preparation for the personal, political and safety realities of entering public life.

4 items in this bundle · Built by the Centre’s learning team · Last updated September 2026

What’s inside

Guide

Family conversation guide

Prepare a clear conversation about purpose, time, responsibilities, privacy and support.

Checklist

Personal safety checklist

Plan travel, digital boundaries, escalation contacts and team protocols before public activity begins.

Worksheet

Support-network map

Identify trusted people for family, legal, campaign, emotional and practical support.

Worksheet

Reservation lookup worksheet

Record the relevant ward or local-body category, official source and verification date.

Why this toolkit is built this way

Women entering public life are often expected to solve political, household, safety and reputation questions at the same time. The toolkit makes those constraints discussable and turns them into named preparations instead of private burdens.

It does not assume one family structure or one route into politics. Each tool is designed to support an honest decision and a safer, stronger support system.

Who this is for

For women considering a first public role, preparing to join a campaign or exploring a future candidacy.
