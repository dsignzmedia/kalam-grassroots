Nominate someone

You know someone.

Every ward has a grassroots leader who already does the work and will never put themselves forward. Empowering them starts with someone else noticing — put their name in.

How it works

1

You tell us about them — 6 fields, 2 minutes.

2

They get a WhatsApp message naming you as the nominator.

3

They land on a personalised Is this for you page.

4

They choose: an info session, or the waiting list.

The first message clearly names the person who made the nomination so the invitation is transparent and easy to decline.

Consent and privacy

The nominee's first message includes an immediate, one-tap "remove me" that erases the record. Both this affirmation and that action are logged. This process is reviewed as part of the privacy policy.

What happens after you submit

"Thank you — we'll reach out to the person you nominated on WhatsApp within 3 days, naming you as the person who nominated them."
