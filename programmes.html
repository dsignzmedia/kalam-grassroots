After the Lab

Then the long road. Fifty more steps.

Becoming an elected representative — in a ward, a local body or beyond — is not one course. It is years of steady work. These are the fifty things leaders keep practising after the Lab. None comes with a promise; each is earned by showing up, week after week.

Steps 1–5

Know your ward

  1. 01Walk every street of your ward
  2. 02Map who lives, works and decides there
  3. 03Learn your ward’s biggest public issues
  4. 04Know your councillor, officials and offices
  5. 05Understand how local budgets are spent

Steps 6–12

Understand the institutions

  1. 06Explain how your local body works
  2. 07Know what each level of government controls
  3. 08Read a ward or panchayat budget
  4. 09Follow a council or gram sabha meeting
  5. 10Understand election timelines and rules
  6. 11Know the model code of conduct
  7. 12Follow one election from notice to result

Steps 13–17

Listen and act on issues

  1. 13Hold structured conversations with residents
  2. 14Turn complaints into diagnosed issues
  3. 15Document one issue from start to outcome
  4. 16Follow up until something changes
  5. 17Keep an honest record of the work you do

Steps 18–23

Build a team

  1. 18Recruit your first volunteers
  2. 19Give every volunteer a clear role
  3. 20Run a steady weekly team rhythm
  4. 21Delegate without losing quality
  5. 22Train someone to take over from you
  6. 23Keep people involved when energy dips

Steps 24–29

Communicate with the public

  1. 24Speak clearly in Tamil and English
  2. 25Tell your story in one minute
  3. 26Write a short, honest message
  4. 27Handle a hostile question calmly
  5. 28Brief a journalist
  6. 29Respond to a crisis

Steps 30–33

Use data responsibly

  1. 30Use voter and issue data responsibly
  2. 31Protect people’s privacy and consent
  3. 32Run a simple digital channel
  4. 33Spot and correct misinformation

Steps 34–38

Stay compliant

  1. 34Understand spending limits and rules
  2. 35Keep clean records of every rupee
  3. 36Know eligibility and disqualification rules
  4. 37Prepare nomination papers correctly
  5. 38Meet every legal deadline

Steps 39–43

Build character

  1. 39Keep your word, even when it costs you
  2. 40Stay calm under pressure
  3. 41Reflect on what went wrong, and fix it
  4. 42Take criticism without giving up
  5. 43Look after your health, family and time

Steps 44–47

Build relationships

  1. 44Build trust across communities
  2. 45Negotiate and build coalitions
  3. 46De-escalate conflict
  4. 47Find a mentor, then mentor someone else

Steps 48–50

Work the field — and keep showing up

  1. 48Plan a week of field work
  2. 49Train polling-day agents
  3. 50Keep showing up, week after week

The horizon

Ready to serve as an elected representative.

Ward. Local body. And beyond. There is no single route and no promised seat — only the work, done consistently.
