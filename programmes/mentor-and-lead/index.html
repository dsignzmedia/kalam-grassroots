Programmes / Leadership in action

100-Day AKF Public Problem-Solving Lab

Continued learning · Field-based
OverviewOutcomesCurriculumExperienceRequirementsFeesApply

Overview

Move from classroom capability to leadership in action.

One hundred days to address a real public problem and produce evidence of responsible grassroots leadership.

This is continued learning, not another classroom course. Fellows diagnose a public challenge, organise people, act, review the results, reflect and adapt with structured support.

Key details

Format
Continued field learning
Duration
100 days
Method
Diagnose · Organise · Act · Review
Support
Mentoring and evidence review
Outcome
Verified public-impact evidence
Next step
Evaluation, certification and responsibility

What you will gain

A public result—and evidence of how you led.

01

Field challenge

Define a real public problem, the people affected and the change you can responsibly pursue.

02

Reflection and adaptation

Review what happened, learn from evidence and change course when the work demands it.

03

Verified impact

Build a portfolio that connects your decisions and organising practice to a public outcome.

The action cycle

A repeatable process keeps the fellow focused on the public problem and the evidence.

01Diagnose

Understand the problem, institutions, stakeholders, lived experience and available evidence.

02Organise

Build the relationships, roles and shared commitment needed to act responsibly.

03Act

Carry out a defined intervention with clear responsibilities, safeguards and measures.

04Review

Examine results, reflect honestly, adapt the approach and document the next responsibility.

Continued learning experience

Support while the real work unfolds.

Fellows stay connected to mentors and peers while leading the field challenge. Reviews are timed to help reflection and adaptation—not to replace local judgement.

  • Public-problem diagnosis
  • Mentoring and peer reflection
  • Evidence and impact review
  • Evaluation, certification and next-responsibility guidance

Requirements

Who this is for

  • You have completed the Foundation Course and Leadership Academy.
  • You can take responsibility for a real public challenge over 100 days.
  • You will document decisions, activity, reflection and evidence honestly.
  • You can participate in mentoring, review and final evaluation.

Free to attend.

No tuition fee.

Programme sessions, feedback and core learning materials are included.
