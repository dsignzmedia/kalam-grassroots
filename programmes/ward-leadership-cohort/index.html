2-Day Foundation Course

Classroom programme · Orientation
OverviewOutcomesCurriculumExperienceRequirementsFeesApply

Overview

Begin with purpose and a clear leadership intent.

A two-day classroom orientation to the Abdul Kalam Fellow leadership journey.

Explore leadership through four schools—Head, Heart, Hand and Compass. Build shared understanding with your cohort and leave with a personal leadership intent that guides your next stage.

Key details

Format
In-person classroom
Duration
2 days
Stage
Orientation
Language
Tamil & English
Location
Neelambur, Coimbatore
Next step
4-Week Leadership Academy

What you will do

Orient yourself before building capability.

01

Discover purpose

Examine the public problem you care about and why you want to take responsibility for it.

02

Build shared understanding

Develop a common language for leadership, service, evidence and accountability.

03

Set leadership intent

Write a clear intent that connects your values to the work and learning ahead.

The Four Schools

Four connected lenses help participants understand the whole practice of leadership.

01Head

Understand institutions, systems, evidence and the decisions a public leader must make.

02Heart

Develop empathy, values, courage and a grounded sense of responsibility to others.

03Hand

Connect intent to practical action, collaboration and disciplined follow-through.

04Compass

Use ethics and purpose to navigate pressure, ambiguity and competing choices.

Programme experience

Two focused days in the classroom.

Short teaching blocks, guided reflection and cohort discussion help participants connect personal purpose with the standards of responsible public leadership.

  • Four Schools learning framework
  • Guided reflection and peer dialogue
  • Personal leadership-intent statement
  • Clear orientation to the full AKF journey

Requirements

Who this is for

  • You want to take responsibility for a public or community problem.
  • No political family, title or previous campaign experience is required.
  • You can attend both classroom days at the Centre in Neelambur, Coimbatore.
  • You are open to reflection, practice and feedback.

The first step.

Orientation before capability building.

Participants who complete the course leave with a leadership intent and a clear understanding of what the four-week Academy will require.
