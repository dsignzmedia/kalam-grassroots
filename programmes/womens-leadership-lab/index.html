Programmes / Women in public leadership

Women's Leadership Lab

Women only · Residential
OverviewOutcomesCurriculumSupportRequirementsFeesWaitlist

Overview

Build political capability with practical support around you.

A focused space for women considering public life—with or without a political family or campaign experience.

The Lab combines leadership practice, peer learning, mentoring and honest planning around safety, care and family responsibilities.

Key details

Format
Residential
Duration
4 days
Language
Tamil
Location
Tamil Nadu
Fee
Free
Status
Waitlist only
Join the waitlist

What you will gain

A stronger voice, a clearer plan and people in your corner.

01

Public confidence

Practise speaking, decision-making and responding under pressure.

02

A support network

Build relationships with women facing similar political and practical barriers.

03

Your next step

Leave with a realistic plan for learning, field work, organising or candidacy.

Curriculum

Two integrated modules connect political capability with the conditions women need to use it.

01Public leadership foundations

Understand institutions, public narrative, local issue work and the standards behind credible leadership.

02Networks, safety and practical readiness

Plan for support, care, family conversations, safety and the resources required for your next step.

Support built in

The practical barriers are part of the programme.

Support can be discussed during enrolment so participants can make an informed and realistic plan.

  • Need-based childcare by prior arrangement
  • Optional family conversation support
  • A woman mentor for programme and field guidance
  • Peer support throughout the Lab

Requirements

Who this is for

  • This programme is open only to women.
  • No political family or previous campaign experience is required.
  • You can attend the complete four-day residential programme.
  • You are considering a serious role in public leadership.

Free to attend.

No tuition fee.

Core learning, programme materials, meals and shared accommodation are included.
