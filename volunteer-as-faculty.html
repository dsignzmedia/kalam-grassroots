Teach / Contribute

Teach, mentor or contribute your thinking.

Share your field experience with people preparing for public life. We welcome practitioners who can teach a useful method, mentor emerging leaders or contribute rigorous public thinking.

Ways to contribute

Choose the depth that fits your time.

Every contribution is anchored in practice that participants can apply.

01

Teach a module

Own a curriculum module on a cohort, in person or hybrid. See programmes for the curriculum shape.

02

Mentor a cohort

Ongoing mentoring of a small group between sessions — the “Lead Others” relationship, applied to faculty.

03

Guest session

A single lecture, workshop or Q&A — the lowest-commitment way to contribute.

04

Contribute your thinking

Write or review a Library resource, a case study or a published essay under the Centre’s name.

Who we’re looking for

Credibility comes from specific work.

We look for subject expertise, lived field experience or a record of thoughtful work in governance, policy, organising and campaigns. Credibility comes from specific work and teachable practice, not titles alone. Meet the current faculty practice areas.
